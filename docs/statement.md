# uji — not yet

Dōgen wrote that time is not something that passes by us: *being is time, and time is being*. Each moment is complete in itself, and the firewood does not become the ash.

Noah Doersing's *uji* draws one line and redraws it every moment, slightly moved. The image is all those moments laid on top of each other. In this fork, the line no longer shares a single moment. Each part of it keeps its own clock. One end has already begun when the other has not yet started. Until its moment arrives, a part of the line is not drawn and does not move. When it begins, it joins a line whose other parts have been travelling for some time.

What appears on paper is the seam between those times: a ring that never closes, and a hard edge where the line hasn't arrived yet. Each piece is decided entirely by its seed number. The plotter decided the rest. Every square and triangle the system proposed folded into its own corners and saturated the paper, and only circles survived the check. The series became a set of ensō: circles drawn in one movement and left open.

*Pen on paper, AxiDraw, A4. Made with a fork of uji by Noah Doersing (MIT, no NFTs).*
