wake

three ways to play an instrument that has no presets and no wall of sliders

A · Direct

centre
radius · drag
rotation origin
scroll on canvas = time · shift-drag = twist

You touch the drawing, not a control. Handles appear on hover: centre, radius, rotation origin, and the "now" edge of the delay. + Immediate. − Can't reach every parameter or show the order of operations.

B · Stack

+ block

The engine's real structure is shown: an ordered list of blocks you drag to reorder. Each block has one big knob, and clicking ∿ opens its time and along signals. + Shows the idea and reaches everything. − More to read.

C · Gesture

You draw one stroke. Its path becomes the seed and its speed and curvature set the blocks; then you watch the wake. + Magical first 10 seconds. − Hard to refine and hard to reproduce on purpose.

Recommendation: B, with A's handles on the canvas. The stack is the idea, so the interface should show it. Direct handles handle the common moves (centre, radius, origin, the "now" edge). C becomes one seed type: "draw your own seed".
